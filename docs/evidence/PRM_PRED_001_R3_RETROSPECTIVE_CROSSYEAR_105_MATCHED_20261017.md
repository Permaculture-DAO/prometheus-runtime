# PRM-PRED-001 — Cross-year retrospective NOAA comparison, 2025 versus 2026

**Evidence date:** 2026-10-17. **Classification:** candidate, noncanonical, read-only external-model evaluation. **Scientific R3 gate: HOLD.**

## Critical interpretation

This is a **calendar-matched retrospective year separation**, not a blinded, preregistered, forward-year holdout. The 2025 period was selected **after examining 2026**. NOAA operational GFS models may have different versions, parameters and forecast errors across years; source metadata does not independently prove model-version comparability. The **forecasts were generated by NOAA**, not PROMETHEUS. The demonstrated PROMETHEUS capability is reproducible audit and refusal to promote unsupported inferences.

The new PRM-PRED-YEAR-TRANSFER-001@0.1 module matches **station + nominal issue month + nominal issue day + lead**, without pooling forecast skill across years. Every unmatched case is excluded on both sides. It emits `prospective_validation=false`, `true_independent_holdout=false`, `scientific_significance_established=false`, `risk_gates_modified=false`.

## Sources and populations

- Historic NOAA GFS 00Z operational 0.25-degree 2m temperature GRIB2 fields, 2025 **February 3–6, May 3–6, September 3–6**, forecast leads +24, +48, +72h; 36 archived forecast fields successfully retrieved.
- NOAA GHCNh archived 2025 station fragments: **Miami USW00012839; Atlanta USW00013874; Jacksonville USW00013889**. Nine timestamped S3 byte-range fragments (source integrity digests retained). S3 GHCNh station data and NOAA model content are externally provided and require independent source and publication-latency verification.
- Candidate observed-temperature filter: blank QC or documented GHCNh source `223` + quality code `1`; other undocumented nonblank QC codes excluded. NOAA primary QC authority and full station quality flags require steward review.
- Baselines: timestamped station persistence 20–180 minutes before 00Z GFS issue (actual public availability **not independently authenticated**) and official NOAA 1991–2020 `HLY-TEMP-NORMAL` for each station, Fahrenheit converted to Celsius, correctly aligned at **UTC−05:00 local standard time**.
- **105 valid 2025 forecast/station/lead events out of 108 potential**. The three excluded cases are all Atlanta, missing a station temperature within ±60 minutes of the target:
  2025-02-03 +72h; 2025-02-04 +48h; 2025-02-05 +24h.
- Comparison 2026 uses the independently stored **432-case** dataset (Feb/May/Jul/Aug/Sep/Oct). To avoid mismatched season/day composition, only the 2026 events that share the 2025 site's issue month/day and lead were retained. **105 calendar-matched pairs** per baseline, 35 per lead; unpaired observations from either year are excluded **symmetrically**.

## Actual result — NOAA GFS MAE in degrees Celsius, matched issue dates only

| Lead | Matched events per year | NOAA GFS 2025 | NOAA GFS 2026 | Change in MAE (2026 minus 2025) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| +24h | 35 | **1.069015** | **1.672792** | +0.603777 |
| +48h | 35 | **1.147830** | **2.554443** | +1.406613 |
| +72h | 35 | **1.781046** | **2.415590** | +0.634544 |

Positive change means the 2026 sampled NOAA GFS errors were higher on the calendar-matched nominal issue day/site/lead pairs. **This does NOT mean GFS has deteriorated, that forecast versions have comparable operational attributes, or that PROMETHEUS can forecast weather.** Weather regimes and sample selection remain material.
